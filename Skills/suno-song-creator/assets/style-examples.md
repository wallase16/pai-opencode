## Style Prompt Techniques

### Basic Format
```
[genre] [subgenre] [mood] [instrumentation]
```

### Examples
- "upbeat pop with synth leads and driving beat"
- "dreamy indie folk with acoustic guitar and soft vocals"
- "aggressive punk rock with distorted guitars"
- "smooth r&b with soulful vocals and light instrumentation"

### Advanced Combinations
- "lo-fi hip hop beats with jazz piano samples"
- "electronic dance with orchestral elements"
- "folk rock with electronic production"
- "classical strings with modern pop structure"

### Rock
