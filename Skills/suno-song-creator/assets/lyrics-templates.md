# Song Lyrics Templates

Use these templates as starting points for your AI-generated songs. Each template includes structure, themes, and examples to help create engaging lyrics.

## Basic Song Structures

### Verse-Chorus-Verse-Chorus-Bridge-Chorus (Pop/Rock)
```
[Verse 1]
Opening line that sets the scene
Second line builds the story
Third line adds emotion/conflict
Fourth line creates anticipation

[Chorus]
Main hook - most memorable part
Supporting line that reinforces theme
Call to action or emotional peak
Repeat or variation of hook

[Verse 2]
Continue the story from verse 1
Add new details or perspective
Build toward emotional climax
Lead back to chorus

[Chorus]
Repeat chorus with possible variation

[Bridge]
Contrast section - different melody/tempo
New perspective or revelation
Build tension for final chorus
Transition back to familiar

[Chorus]
Final chorus - full energy
Repeat hook for memorable ending
Fade out or strong finish
```

### Simple Verse-Chorus (Folk/Country)
```
[Verse 1]
Tell the story simply
Paint a picture with words
Keep it conversational
Build emotional connection

[Chorus]
Main message or feeling
Repeat for memorability
Keep it simple and true
Make it singable

[Verse 2]
Continue the narrative
Add depth to characters
Show rather than tell
Lead to emotional payoff

[Chorus]
Repeat with feeling
Emphasize key emotions
End on a resonant note
```

## Writing Tips

### Structure Your Lyrics
1. **Hook First:** Start with the most memorable line
2. **Story Arc:** Build from setup to climax to resolution
3. **Repetition:** Use chorus for emotional reinforcement
4. **Contrast:** Bridge should differ from verses/chorus

### Emotional Flow
- **Verse:** Tell the story, set the scene
- **Chorus:** Deliver the emotional payload
- **Bridge:** Provide contrast or revelation

### Word Choice
- **Concrete Imagery:** Use sensory details
- **Rhythm:** Consider syllable count and flow
- **Rhyme Scheme:** AABB, ABAB, or freestyle
- **Emotional Words:** Choose words that evoke feeling

### Theme Ideas
- Love and relationships
- Personal growth and change
- Social issues and commentary
- Nature and seasons
- Dreams and aspirations
- Friendship and connection
- Overcoming adversity
- Celebration and joy

Remember: These are starting points. Feel free to modify, combine, or create your own structures. The best songs come from personal experiences and genuine emotions!
